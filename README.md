### 🌍 Overview

*EleTect* is a modular, AI-powered ecosystem of devices designed to promote *human–wildlife coexistence*, prevent conflicts, and protect both nature and communities.  
Built using *TinyML, **LoRa, and **bio-inspired design principles, EleTect redefines conservation technology through **low-power, scalable, and sustainable innovation*.

This repository covers the complete *EleTect 2.0 system* and its modular extensions — forming a connected network of intelligent devices that monitor, detect, deter, and communicate in real-time.

## 🧩 Ecosystem Lineup

### *1️⃣ EleTect 2.0 – The Core System*
> The central AI-powered unit for *human–elephant conflict mitigation*.

- Dual *vision + acoustic AI* detection for real-time elephant identification.  
- *Bio-inspired deterrent* using honeybee swarm sounds.  
- Detects *illegal human activities* (gunshots, chainsaws, vehicles).  
- *Solar-powered, **LoRa-enabled, and capable of **local data logging* (SD + RTC).  
- Acts as the *hub* of the entire EleTect ecosystem.  

📘 Detailed Documentation: [EleTect 2.0 Hackster Project](https://www.hackster.io/abhinav123krish/eletect-2-0-76abc2)

---

### *2️⃣ EleTect Razor – Modular Boar Deterrent*
> A plug-and-play module that attaches to EleTect 2.0.

- High-intensity *strobe lights* and *ultrasonic deterrence*.  
- EEPROM-based auto-recognition for instant configuration.  
- Designed to adapt EleTect 2.0 for wild boar management.  

---

### *3️⃣ EleTect 1.5 – Smarter Roads, Safer Journeys*
> Intelligent wildlife signage for road safety near forest areas.

- Deployed along *elephant corridors (Aana Thaara)* using local knowledge for placement.  
- Communicates with detection nodes via *LoRa*.  
- *Solar-powered* system that warns vehicles in real-time.  
- Can be extended to detect *other large animals* in crossings.  

📘 Detailed Documentation: [EleTect 1.5 Hackster Project](https://www.hackster.io/abhinav123krish/eletect-1-5-smarter-warnings-safer-journeys-1597d3)

---

### *4️⃣ EleTect Mini – Compact Multi-Animal Deterrent*
> A lightweight, portable solution for farms and households.

- Built using *XIAO ESP32S3 + TinyML*.  
- *Rotary mode selector* for species-specific deterrence (birds, dogs, monkeys, squirrels, etc.).  
- Dual charging: *USB Type-C + Solar*.  
- Features dual “eye” strobes and an intelligent speaker for deterrence.  

---

### *5️⃣ EleTect Echo – Acoustic Surveillance Node*
> The forest’s “ears” — monitoring illegal activity 24/7.

- Detects *gunshots, logging, vehicle movement*, and unusual sounds.  
- Logs acoustic data to SD with timestamps (RTC-based).  
- Transmits alerts via *LoRa MeshStatic* to ranger units.  

---

### *6️⃣ EleTect Ember – Early Wildfire Detection*
> A stackable add-on for Echo, creating a complete forest monitoring suite.

- Detects *smoke, flame, temperature, and **gas anomalies*.  
- Early fire alerts sent to connected *Echo nodes* and *EleTect Link*.  
- Modular “Lego-style” design — Echo and Ember interlock seamlessly.  

---

### *7️⃣ EleTect Link – Ranger Communication Device*
> Handheld, smart, and always connected.

- Connects to EleTect 2.0, Echo, and Ember via *LoRa MeshStatic*.  
- Displays real-time alerts on an *OLED/e-paper screen*.  
- Supports *SOS signaling, **vibration, and **buzzer alerts*.  
- Works fully *offline*, enabling communication deep inside forests.  

---

## 🧠 Innovation Highlights

- ✅ *TinyML + IoT Fusion* – Real-time detection on low-power devices.  
- 🌿 *Bio-inspired Design* – Honeybee deterrence derived from natural elephant behavior.  
- ☀ *Solar-Powered Systems* – Fully off-grid and sustainable.  
- 🔗 *Modular Architecture* – Attach, adapt, and scale easily.  
- 🛰 *LoRa + Mesh Communication* – Long-range, internet-independent alerts.  
- 🧩 *Cross-Compatible Modules* – All devices interconnect as a unified network.
